# Product

## Register

product

## Users

RIMCO Studio is an internal beta used by a small team of two to five RIMCO Motors staff in Abidjan. Collaborators create video drafts, validators review and approve them, and administrators manage users, brand assets, provider models, pricing, and budgets. The team works in French and needs the full workflow to be operable from a browser.

## Product Purpose

RIMCO Studio turns a real, approved vehicle photo and a short creative brief into a five-second branded video. The team can revise versions, add the approved logo and optional audio, submit the final result for approval, and download it only after approval. The product must make the vehicle reference, approval state, and charged price clear while administrators control real generation spend under a €20 cap.

## Brand Personality

Expert, reassuring, premium. The interface should make a small production team feel in control of a capable, dependable creative tool. Product language is concise, professional French.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic decorative SaaS dashboards, gratuitous card grids, playful AI imagery, and interfaces that hide important generation costs or approval requirements behind ambiguous controls.

## Design Principles

- Preserve the selected vehicle's real identity by grounding generation in an approved reference photo.
- Make each workflow state and required next action clear in French.
- Show the user-facing price before generation and reserve real EUR cost behind a hard budget ceiling.
- Keep collaboration, approvals, and sensitive admin information separated by role.
- Keep brand identity and pricing configurable from the database for future marques.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. All workflows must support keyboard navigation, visible focus, labels and errors that do not rely on color alone, and reduced-motion preferences.
